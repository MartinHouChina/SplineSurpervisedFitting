The following equations specify the main implemented terms; enabled weights
and optional branches must be exported from each checkpoint's configuration.
They are not a replacement objective used only for presentation.
For error $e$ and tolerance $\varepsilon$, the base fit penalty is
\begin{equation}
 \phi(e,\varepsilon)=\log(1+e/\varepsilon+10^{-12})
 +[\log(e/\varepsilon+10^{-12})]_+.
\end{equation}
The feasibility-aware option reduces its weight to $w_f$ below a safety
margin $a\varepsilon$ and subtracts $(1-w_f)\log(1+a+10^{-12})$
above that margin, preserving continuity for the configured $a\leq1$.
Batch aggregation is the mean plus a weighted upper-tail mean, emphasizing
hard curves rather than only the average. Denote that aggregation by
$\mathcal A$ and the configured fit penalty by $\phi_f$.
The joint selected-fit term averages the actual deployment mask and the
teacher-selected mask after decoding both:
\begin{equation}
 L_{\rm sel}=\tfrac12\mathcal A[\phi_f(e_{\rm deploy},\varepsilon)]
             +\tfrac12\mathcal A[\phi_f(e_{\rm teacher},\varepsilon)].
\end{equation}
The finite teacher search compares feasible masks by increasing cardinality
and then error. If no evaluated mask is feasible, it selects the lowest-error
alternative; such a fallback must not be counted as a feasible teacher.
The basic feasibility-first subset cost is
\begin{equation}
 J(S)=\begin{cases}
 (|S|+0.25\min(e_S/\varepsilon,1))/(K_c+1),&e_S\leq\varepsilon,\\
 2+[\log(e_S/\varepsilon+10^{-12})]_+,&e_S>\varepsilon.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
Additional configured search costs or budgets must be reported separately.

For teacher mask $y_j$ and selector probability $p_j$, the unaligned mask
distillation term is weighted binary cross entropy:
\begin{equation}
 L_{\rm mask}=-\frac1{K_c}\sum_j[1+(w_+-1)y_j]
 [y_j\log p_j+(1-y_j)\log(1-p_j)].
\end{equation}
If count-reserve alignment is enabled, the implemented soft aligned targets
replace $y_j$. Requested count score $s$ is trained toward
$k^*=\max(|S^*|-0.25,0)$, inside the relevant ceiling interval:
\begin{equation}
 L_{\rm count}=\operatorname{SmoothL1}(\log(1+\max(s,0)),\log(1+k^*)).
\end{equation}
Positive--negative teacher pairs also receive a ranking term
$\operatorname{softplus}(\mu-\ell_j+\ell_k)$, where $y_j=1$, $y_k=0$,
and $\ell$ are selector logits. Ordered assignment and knot-position labels
are meaningful only in a shared parameterization. Label-free procedural
training examples do not receive invented source-knot supervision.

Writing each enabled term explicitly, the implemented joint objective is
\begin{align}
 L={}&\lambda_{\rm sel}L_{\rm sel}+\lambda_dL_d
 +\lambda_\pi L_\pi+\lambda_mL_{\rm mask}+\lambda_cL_{\rm count}
 +\lambda_rL_{\rm rank}+\lambda_hL_{\rm entropy}
 +s_c\lambda_KL_{\rm complexity}\nonumber\\
 &+\lambda_{sc}L_{\rm sourceCount}+\lambda_{oc}L_{\rm overCount}
 +\lambda_tL_{\rm parameter}+\lambda_{cv}L_{\rm coverage}
 +\lambda_uL_{\rm position}+\lambda_bL_{\rm boundary}\nonumber\\
 &+\lambda_{cf}L_{\rm chordCounterfactual}+\lambda_lL_{\rm local}
 +\lambda_pL_{\rm peak}+\lambda_oL_{\rm ordered}
 +\lambda_gL_{\rm teacherGeometry}+\lambda_{cm}L_{\rm compactMask}.
\end{align}
Here $L_\pi$ denotes stochastic-subset policy learning, $L_d$ dense proposal
fit, and $s_c$ the scheduled complexity scale. Source-count and excess-count
penalties use feasibility-dependent eligibility, so a true-count label does
not force further pruning of an already infeasible subset. The proposal stage
uses its proposal-specific branch rather than the entire joint sum. The source
of truth is \texttt{V16SubsetLoss.forward}; a zero coefficient disables a term.
Teacher labels are detached, while redecoded continuous geometry and refits
provide geometry gradients. No ordinary gradient through hard Top-$K$ is
assumed. Online search budgets affect training time but are absent at inference.
